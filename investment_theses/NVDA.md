# NVDA
## Thesis
- Dominant merchant AI accelerator platform commanding ~$4.58T market cap on FY26 revenue of $215.9B (+65.5% YoY) and $102.7B operating cash flow
- Full-stack moat: CUDA software lock-in, NVLink rack-scale systems, Mellanox networking, and DPUs create prohibitive switching costs that commodity chip alternatives cannot replicate
- Each GPU generation (Hopper → Blackwell → Vera Rubin) commands a price premium on cost-per-result, not cost-per-chip, sustaining gross margins recovering to 75.0% in Q4 FY26
- $95.2B in long-dated foundry, HBM, and packaging commitments pre-empts competitor access to supply at scale
- Net-cash balance sheet ($62.6B liquidity vs. $8.5B debt); management formalized ~50% FCF return target implying $200B+ in buybacks and dividends across CY26–27
- Founder-CEO Jensen Huang has navigated every material shock (FY23 inventory, FY25 Blackwell yield, FY26 H20 export charge) with fast disclosure and minimal lasting financial damage

## Key Catalysts
- **Vera Rubin ramp (2H 2026):** Volume production of NVL72 rack-scale system; OpenAI LOI for first gigawatt of Vera Rubin systems a near-term demand floor
- **Q1 FY27 earnings (May 20, 2026):** Guided $78.0B ±2% with zero China DC compute revenue assumed; beat/raise cycle could re-rate multiple
- **Hyperscaler capex expansion:** GOOGL $175–185B, AMZN $128B+, META $72B, MSFT $34.9B/quarter all flowing disproportionately to NVIDIA silicon through CY27
- **$1T+ Blackwell+Rubin backlog:** Disclosed at GTC March 2026 as a floor through CY27, providing multi-year revenue visibility
- **Adjacent revenue monetization:** Autonomous vehicles (Uber DRIVE, 28 cities from 2027), robotics, and enterprise AI software not yet material in reported numbers
- **Dynamo inference framework:** Claims up to 30x throughput improvement on Blackwell for reasoning workloads, reinforcing GPU relevance in inference alongside training

## Risk Factors
- **Customer concentration:** Two customers represent 22% and 14% of FY26 revenue respectively; loss or capex pullback by either is a material near-term earnings risk
- **U.S. export controls:** China DC compute revenue assumed zero in Q1 FY27 guide; further restrictions on remaining addressable geographies (e.g., Middle East) could compound revenue loss beyond the $4.5B H20 charge already absorbed
- **AI capex monetization risk:** If hyperscaler ROI on AI infrastructure disappoints, capex commitments could pull back sharply while NVIDIA holds $95.2B in supply obligations due substantially through FY27 — high severity, medium probability
- **Hyperscaler in-house silicon:** Broadcom-designed custom ASICs and internal chip programs (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium, Meta MTIA) structurally erode merchant GPU share at the largest buyers over a 3–5 year horizon
- **Supply chain concentration:** Single-source dependencies on TSMC (leading-edge logic), SK hynix/Micron (HBM), and CoWoS advanced packaging; Taiwan geopolitical risk is low probability but existential severity
- **Gross margin volatility:** New architecture transitions and supply commitment write-downs (precedents: $1.22B FY23, $4.5B FY26) can compress margins materially in transition quarters
- **Inference shift to ASICs:** As inference workloads scale and stabilize, purpose-built low-power ASICs may capture percentage share; absolute GPU demand likely still grows but mix headwinds are real
