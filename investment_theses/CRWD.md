# CRWD

## Thesis
- CrowdStrike is the #1 independent endpoint security platform with ~$4.24B in FY2026 revenue (~96% subscription), operating in a ~$225B TAM growing at ~12% CAGR
- Cloud-native Falcon platform with 28+ modules creates compounding switching costs; net retention consistently above 120% with 65%+ of customers on 5+ modules
- Data moat is self-reinforcing: trillions of security events processed weekly improve AI/ML detection for all customers, widening the gap vs. legacy vendors
- "Land and expand" motion (endpoint → cloud security → identity → SIEM) structurally expands wallet share per customer without proportional CAC
- Post-July 2024 outage recovery arrived a quarter ahead of guidance; record net new ARR in FY2026 Q3 and record FCF validate platform durability and customer loyalty
- Management demonstrated capital discipline: conservative guidance, transparent CCP cost quantification, 5% workforce reduction to expand margins, $1B buyback authorized
- Stock is ~37% off all-time highs, creating an attractive risk/reward entry for a competitively dominant compounder

## Key Catalysts
- **ARR reacceleration**: FY2026 Q3 record net new ARR with upward guidance revision (revenue ~$4.80B, non-GAAP EPS ~$3.71) signals durable re-inflection
- **Falcon Flex adoption**: Flexible licensing driving larger, multi-year platform consolidation deals and accelerating multi-module attach
- **Next-gen SIEM (LogScale) and identity protection**: Both markets growing >20% CAGR and largely unpenetrated within CRWD's existing customer base — significant upsell runway
- **Cloud security (CNAPP/CSPM)**: Fastest-growing security segment at ~25% CAGR per Gartner; CRWD expanding aggressively with an architectural advantage
- **FCF margin expansion**: Capital-light SaaS model targeting 35%+ FCF margins; strategic workforce realignment accelerates path to profitability milestones
- **AI-driven threat escalation**: Sophisticated AI-powered attacks structurally increase enterprise willingness to pay for best-of-breed real-time detection

## Risk Factors
- **Legal overhang**: Delta Air Lines litigation and outstanding DOJ/SEC information requests from the July 2024 outage remain unresolved; financial exposure uncertain
- **Microsoft bundling**: Defender for Endpoint gaining SMB/mid-market share via aggressive bundling with M365; pricing pressure risk on entry-level tiers
- **Valuation sensitivity**: Premium multiple (~20x NTM revenue) requires sustained 20%+ growth; any deceleration would compress the multiple disproportionately
- **Adjacent market execution**: SIEM, identity, and cloud security pits CRWD against entrenched incumbents (Splunk/Google, SailPoint, Wiz/Palo Alto); win rates unproven at scale
- **Macro-driven deal elongation**: Enterprise security budgets are resilient but not immune; large platform consolidation deals may slip in a recessionary environment
- **Incident recurrence risk**: A second software update failure would be materially more damaging to retention and brand than the July 2024 event
