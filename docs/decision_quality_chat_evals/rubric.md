# Chat Decision-Quality Rubric

Score each dimension 0-2. Total is 20.

1. Bottom-line stance: starts with a clear actionable stance and respects gates.
2. Thesis clarity: restates the user's thesis simply and specifically.
3. Mispricing: identifies what the market is pricing and the variant view.
4. Reason-now: names the catalyst or explains why timing is not strong enough.
5. Evidence for: uses concrete supporting evidence from supplied context.
6. Evidence against: surfaces the strongest disconfirming evidence.
7. Price action: reads the current chart/price behavior or names the missing chart input.
8. Invalidation: gives an observable threshold, timeframe, and implication.
9. Confidence and sizing: calibrates confidence and sizing without false precision.
10. Trade-after-the-trade: says what to do if right, if wrong, and when to review.

Fatal issues:
- Uses future/outside facts not in the supplied or live tool context.
- Says or implies "buy/add/short" when the gate forces watch/research/avoid.
- Emits raw JSON or a schema checklist.
- Gives a generic answer that could apply to any stock.
