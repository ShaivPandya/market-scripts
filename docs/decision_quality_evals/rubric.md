# Decision Quality Rubric

Score each dimension from `0` to `2`.

- `0` - missing, generic, circular, or unsupported.
- `1` - partially useful but incomplete, vague, or weakly supported.
- `2` - concrete, decision-useful, and supported by the provided inputs.

## Dimensions

1. **Thesis clarity**
   The decision has a simple, testable thesis. It is not just a list of positives.

2. **Mispricing**
   The output explains what consensus likely believes, what the variant view is, and why price may not reflect that future.

3. **Catalyst / reason-now**
   The output explains why the decision matters now, not merely why the asset is interesting.

4. **Evidence quality**
   The output separates evidence for the thesis from assumptions, stale facts, or unsupported claims.

5. **Disconfirming evidence**
   The output names the strongest opposing evidence or missing facts. It does not hide uncertainty.

6. **Invalidation**
   The output gives observable invalidation with metric/event, threshold, timeframe, and implication.

7. **Price action / market behavior**
   The output treats price action and news response as evidence, or explicitly names the missing price-action input.

8. **Actionability discipline**
   The output distinguishes actionable decisions from watch/research/do_nothing when catalyst, evidence, timing, or data quality is insufficient.

9. **Confidence calibration**
   Confidence matches evidence quality. Missing inputs, conflicting evidence, stale data, or weak invalidation reduce confidence.

10. **Sizing and risk context**
    If an action is recommended, the output addresses liquidity, volatility, concentration, portfolio fit, and add/reduce conditions.

## Suggested Aggregate Score

Add the ten dimensions for a `0-20` total:

- `17-20` - strong decision process
- `13-16` - usable but needs review
- `9-12` - incomplete; should not drive action
- `0-8` - poor process
